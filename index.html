Started because collection wasn't reliable

Ecolocate was founded by Becken Makobi Anne Ferrida after seeing how unreliable formal waste collection was for households across Douala's neighbourhoods — bins left uncollected for weeks, informal dumping, and streets that trucks simply couldn't reach.

Today, one tricycle and a trained agent serve paying subscribers every week, with the plan to grow into more neighbourhoods and hire more local agents as the business grows.

Our mission

Reliable, affordable doorstep waste collection for every Douala neighbourhood — no matter how narrow the street.
